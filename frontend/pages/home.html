FAQ

Common questions about using AIP for study and visa preparation.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You need an account to save your profile, checklist and session requests.

Is AIP an official government platform?

No. AIP is a student preparation platform and does not replace official Austrian immigration authorities.

Can I search Austrian universities?

Yes. University search is connected to an external university API.

Can I request an online session?

Yes. You can request a session from your dashboard and follow its status.
